The hollow body is the shape your torso holds during almost every calisthenics skill — the
handstand, the front lever, a strict pull-up. It means **ribs down, lower back flat,
everything braced**.

## The position

Lie on your back. Press your lower back into the floor so there is **no gap**. Lift your
shoulder blades and legs a few inches off the ground, arms overhead. If your back arches,
you've gone too far — raise the arms and legs higher until the low back stays flat.

## The progression

1. **Tuck hollow** — knees tucked, arms by your sides. Build to 3×40 s.
2. **One leg extended** — alternate. 3×30 s.
3. **Legs straight, arms by sides.** 3×30 s.
4. **Full hollow** — legs straight and together, arms overhead. *Target: 3×30 s.*
5. **Hollow rocks** — same shape, rock head-to-toe from the mid-back. 3×15.

## Why it matters

A leaking hollow is why beginners "banana" in a handstand and swing in a pull-up. Fix it on
the floor first — it is free progress that transfers to everything.

## The brace, everywhere

The same brace applies standing: **ribs stacked over hips, glutes lightly on, 360° of
abdominal tension** (as if about to be poked in the stomach). This is the default trunk
position for supports, levers and presses.
