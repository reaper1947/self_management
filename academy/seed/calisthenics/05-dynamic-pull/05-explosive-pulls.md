Explosive (plyometric) pulling builds the rate of force development that muscle-ups,
high pull-ups and bar work need — and it's a fun way to test progress.

## The progression

1. **Explosive pull-ups** — pull as high and fast as possible, aiming for chest, then
   sternum, then lower-ribs to the bar. 5×3, full rest.
2. **Chest-to-bar pull-ups** — strict, chest actually contacts the bar. 4×5.
3. **Clapping / release pull-ups** — pull high enough to briefly release the bar (over a
   safe setup). 5×2.
4. **High bar / catch drills** — pull, release, re-catch higher. Advanced, spotter or mats.

## Form cues

- **Full range first** — a fast half-rep is worthless. Dead hang → high as possible.
- **Brace the whole body** — no leg kick. The power comes from the lats and a violent
  scapular pull.
- Keep volume low and quality high; this is nervous-system work, not a burnout finisher.

## Programming

Once a week, early in the session when fresh. 12–18 total explosive reps. Pair with strict
strength work on other days.

## Self-check

- [ ] Strict chest-to-bar pull-ups, 4×5
- [ ] Explosive pull-ups reaching lower-rib/sternum height, 5×3
- [ ] Body stays braced — no kipping — on every rep
