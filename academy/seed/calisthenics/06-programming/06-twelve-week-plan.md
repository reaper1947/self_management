Everything in this course, assembled into twelve weeks you can actually run.

Print it. Write in it. Cross things out and change them — that is what makes it
your plan rather than a document.

## Before week 1

1. Do the **assessment** — all seven tests.
2. Pick **one static focus** and **one dynamic focus** from your lowest scores.
3. Pick a **template** from the weekly split lesson.
4. Put the **deload weeks (4, 8, 12)** in your calendar now.
5. Start a **log**: date, exercise, sets, reps or seconds, and one line of notes.

## The shape

| Weeks | Block | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | Accumulation | add sets and seconds, ~10% a week |
| 4 | Deload | halve the sets |
| 5–7 | Intensification | harder rungs, volume back to week 1 levels |
| 8 | Deload | halve the sets |
| 9–11 | Peak | best rungs, low volume, high quality |
| 12 | Test | re-assess all seven, fresh |

## Week by week

### Weeks 1–3 — accumulation

The job is **volume**, not heroics. You should finish every session feeling you
could have done one more set.

- statics: 4 → 5 → 6 sets, at a rung you hold comfortably
- dynamics: 3 → 4 → 4 sets, adding a rep per set each week
- handstand: 10–15 min, every training day
- joint prep: every session
- maintenance block: twice a week

End of week 3: your total volume should be about 20% above week 1. Not 50%.

### Week 4 — deload

Half the sets. Same movements, same rungs. Keep handstand practice and joint prep
untouched.

You will feel like you are wasting a week. You are not — this is the week the
previous three become strength.

### Weeks 5–7 — intensification

Now move a rung, and let volume come back down to accommodate it.

- statics: move to the next rung, back to 4 sets
- dynamics: next rung, or add load, back to 3 sets
- explosive: add one session a week
- handstand: unchanged

If a rung turns out to be too big a step, use an intermediate — one leg, a half
position, a band. There is always a rung between two rungs.

### Week 8 — deload

Half the sets again.

### Weeks 9–11 — peak

Quality over quantity. Fewer sets, better ones.

- statics: best rung, 3–4 sets, every one clean
- dynamics: heaviest or hardest variation, 3 sets
- cut accessory volume by a third
- handstand: unchanged

### Week 12 — test

- two easy days first
- re-run all seven assessment tests, same order, same warm-up
- film your best static hold and your best dynamic set
- write the numbers next to your week-0 numbers

Then take three or four genuinely easy days before starting the next cycle.

## A worked example

Someone who assessed at: 25 s dead hang, 8 push-ups, 0 pull-ups, 20 s hollow,
15 s support hold, limited shoulder extension, wrists fine.

**Focuses:** static = support hold → L-sit. dynamic = pull-up negatives.

**Weeks 1–3.** Support hold 4→6 sets of 15 s. Pull-up negatives 3×5 at 5 s.
Push-ups 3×8. Hollow holds 3×20 s. German hang rung 1 every session, because
shoulder extension is the gate on module 3.

**Week 4.** Everything halved.

**Weeks 5–7.** Support hold is now 30 s, so move to tuck L-sit, 4 sets of 15 s.
Negatives are easy at 5 s, so band-assisted pull-ups 3×5 plus one honest single
attempt every other day. Push-ups to 3×12.

**Week 8.** Halved.

**Weeks 9–11.** Tuck L-sit 4×20 s, reaching for advanced tuck. Band-assisted
pull-ups with a lighter band. First unassisted rep is likely somewhere in here.

**Week 12.** Re-test. Expect roughly: 40 s dead hang, 15 push-ups, 1–3 pull-ups,
40 s hollow, 30 s support hold. That is a very normal twelve weeks and it is a
real change.

## The log

Keep it stupid and keep it forever:

```
2026-03-14  PULL
  prep 10min
  tuck FL     5 x 12s      (last set sagged - stop at 4 next time)
  pull-ups    4 x 6        (+2 reps vs last week)
  rows        3 x 10
  straight-arm total this week: 480s   (+9% on 440s)
```

That last line is the one that keeps your elbows healthy.

## After twelve weeks

Re-run the whole thing with new focuses, chosen from your new lowest scores. The
structure does not change. The rungs do.

Four cycles is a year, and a year of this — done unremarkably, with the deloads
actually taken — takes most people from "cannot do a pull-up" to "muscle-up in
sight, solid tuck front lever, thirty-second handstand."

Not because the programme is clever. Because it is the same programme in month
eleven as it was in month one.
