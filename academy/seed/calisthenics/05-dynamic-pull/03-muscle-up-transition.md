The muscle-up is a pull-up, a transition, and a dip stitched together. The **transition**
— getting from "chest at the bar" to "hands pressing down on top" — is what everyone gets
stuck on, and it's trainable in isolation.

## Prerequisites

- ~8–10 strict pull-ups **and** ~8 strict dips (or straight-bar dips).
- Comfortable pulling **explosively** to sternum/nipple height on a bar.

## Transition drills

1. **Straight-bar support + forward roll-over** — from a straight-bar support, slowly lower
   the chest forward toward the bar and back. Learn the top position. 3×5.
2. **Band-assisted transitions** — thick band over the bar, foot in it; pull high, then
   drive the elbows up and over the bar into support. 3×5.
3. **Jumping muscle-ups** — from a box, use a small jump to take you through the sticking
   point; focus on a *fast* transition and a clean catch. 4×3.
4. **Negative muscle-ups** — start in straight-arm support on the bar, lower slowly through
   the transition into a dead hang. 4×2, 4–5 s each. **This is the key exercise.**

## The cue that unlocks it

**"Pull the bar to your hips, then punch your hands down."** As you reach peak pull height,
aggressively lean the chest over the bar and drive the hands from in-front to on-top. Speed
through the transition — hesitation kills it.

## Self-check

- [ ] Explosive pull-ups to sternum height, 3×3
- [ ] 4×2 slow negative muscle-ups through a controlled transition
- [ ] Comfortable and stable in a straight-bar support, leaning forward
