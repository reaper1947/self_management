Now assemble the full rep and clean it up — no chicken-wing, no kip, both hands together.

## Building the first rep

Work in this order, dropping assistance as each gets solid:

1. **False grip** (optional on bar, useful on rings) — wrist over the bar so the transition
   distance is shorter. Build false-grip hang time, 3×20 s.
2. **Explosive pull → fast transition → press out.** Do singles with full rest. 5×1.
3. If you catch in a **chicken-wing** (one elbow up first): slow down, do more negatives,
   and cue "both elbows up together."
4. **Kip vs strict:** a small hip drive is fine for your first ever rep, but immediately
   train toward **strict** — dead hang, no leg swing.

## Cleaning it up

- **Both hands transition together.** Film from the front.
- **No excessive lean-back** on the pull — stay under the bar, then explode.
- **Controlled lower** — reverse the whole movement slowly. This builds the strength that
  makes the concentric easy and protects the shoulders.

## Programming

2×/week: 5–8 total quality reps (singles/doubles, long rest) + transition negatives +
straight-bar explosive dips. Deload every 4–6 weeks — the muscle-up is hard on the elbows.

## Progressions from here

Strict muscle-up → **slow muscle-up** → **weighted muscle-up** → **ring muscle-up** →
**wide / L-sit muscle-up**.
