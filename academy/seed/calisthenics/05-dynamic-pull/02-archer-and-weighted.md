Once you own 3×8–10 strict pull-ups, two paths add strength: **load** (weighted) and
**unilateral leverage** (archer → one-arm).

## Weighted pull-ups

- Use a dip belt or hold a dumbbell between the feet.
- **Rep ranges:** 4–6 sets of 3–6 reps for strength. Full dead hang, chin clear, controlled.
- **Progression:** add ~2.5 kg every 1–2 weeks, or add a rep then reset weight up.
- Milestone: a **bodyweight-added pull-up** (e.g. +40 kg at 70 kg BW) is genuinely strong.

## Archer pull-ups → one-arm

1. **Archer pull-up** — pull to one hand while the other arm stays straight along the bar,
   only assisting. Alternate sides. 3×4 each.
2. **Assisted one-arm pull-up** — free hand grips the working wrist or a towel, giving less
   help over time. 3×3 each.
3. **One-arm negative** — from the top, one hand, lower over 5+ s. 3×2 each.
4. **One-arm pull-up** — the standard. Years of consistent work.

## Form cues

- Archer: keep the assisting arm **straight** — if it bends, it's doing too much.
- Expect **elbow and biceps-tendon** load to spike — ramp slowly, warm up thoroughly.
- Brace hard against rotation; the torso wants to twist.

## Self-check

- [ ] Weighted pull-up with +25% bodyweight for 3 reps, **or**
- [ ] 3×4 clean archer pull-ups each side
- [ ] Elbows pain-free with the added load
