Calisthenics loads your wrists, elbows and shoulders in ranges that gym training never
touches. Ten minutes of preparation before every session keeps you training for years.

## Wrists (before any push / handstand work)

- **Wrist rocks** — on all fours, hands flat, rock forward and back. 2×20.
- **Palm-down and palm-up rocks** — fingers pointing at knees, then away, then out. 10 each.
- **Fist and finger-tip holds** — 20 s each to build the tissue for weight on the hands.

## Elbows (before any straight-arm work — levers, planche)

- **Straight-arm band pull-aparts** — 2×15, elbows locked.
- **Weighted or bodyweight elbow flexion/extension** through full range, slow. 2×10.
- Ramp straight-arm loads by **no more than ~10% a week**. Elbows warn late and heal slow.

## Shoulders (before pulling / german hang / dips)

- **Scapular push-ups** and **scapular pulls** — 2×10 each. Own protraction and depression.
- **Band dislocates** — wide grip, slow, 2×10. Builds the overhead and behind-the-back range.
- **Prone Y-T-W raises** — 2×8 each for the mid- and lower-trap.

## The daily minimum

Wrist rocks · scapular pulls · scapular push-ups · band dislocates · 30 s active hang.
Under 5 minutes. Do it even on rest days.

## Self-check

- [ ] I have a fixed 5–10 min prep routine I do every session
- [ ] Wrists tolerate bodyweight on the hands without pain
- [ ] I can do 10 slow band dislocates through full range
