Lesson 1 · The hollow body

Core position · goal: 30-second hold

Video: hollow body setup & common errors

The hollow body is the shape your torso holds during almost every calisthenics skill — the handstand, the front lever, a strict pull-up. It means ribs down, lower back flat to the floor, everything braced.

How to do it: lie on your back, press your lower back into the floor so there's no gap. Lift your shoulder blades and your legs a few inches off the ground, arms overhead. If your back arches, you've gone too far — raise the arms and legs higher until it stays flat.

  • Start with knees tucked and arms by your sides (easiest).
  • Progress to straight legs, then arms overhead (hardest).
  • Never let the lower back lift off the floor — that's the whole point of the drill.

Why it matters

A leaking hollow position is why beginners "banana" in a handstand and swing in a pull-up. Fix it on the floor first — it's free progress.

Lesson 2 · The active hang & scapular control

Shoulder position · goal: 3 × 8 scapular pulls

A passive hang is dangling from your joints. An active hang is holding the bar with your shoulders switched on — blades pulled down and back. Every pull starts from here.

  1. Hang from the bar, arms straight.
  2. Without bending your elbows, pull your shoulder blades down — your body rises an inch and your chest lifts.
  3. Hold 1 second, then lower slowly to a full passive hang. That's one scapular pull.
If you can…Then…
Barely hold 10 sGrip first: 3 daily max hangs, add 5 s/week
Hang 30 s but can't feel your backScapular pulls every session until it clicks
Do 8 controlled scapular pullsYou're ready for the pull-up progression (Module 2)

Lesson 3 · Self-assessment & your 2-week plan

Find your level, then train it

Test each of these once, fresh, with good form. Write down the number — it's your baseline.

TestBeginnerIntermediate
Push-ups (chest to fist height)< 1010–25
Dead hang< 30 s30–60 s
Hollow hold< 20 s20–45 s
Bodyweight squats< 2020–40

2-week foundation plan — 3 short sessions per week:

  • Day A: 3 × hollow hold (max) · 3 × 8 scapular pulls · 3 × 10 incline push-ups
  • Day B: 3 × max dead hang · 3 × 8 slow squats · 2 × 30 s plank
  • Day C: repeat Day A, add one rep or 5 seconds where you can

Rule

Progress one variable per week: +5 s on holds, +1–2 reps on movements, or a harder variation. Small and consistent beats hard and sporadic every time.
